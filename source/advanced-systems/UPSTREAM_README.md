[h1]Pontifex: Advanced Systems[/h1]

Advanced Systems is a collection of battlefield protection and sensing tools built for vehicle crews, mission makers, and Zeus operators. It currently focuses on survivability and artillery awareness through modular systems that can be enabled at mission start or during live operations.

[h2]Systems Included[/h2]
[list]
[*]LORICA Active Protection System (APS) for synced vehicles
[*]Hard-kill interception against incoming rockets and missiles
[*]Soft-kill fallback behavior that uses vehicle fuel as a limited defensive resource
[*]Experimental anti-drone protection and voiced status feedback through ACE interactions
[*]Counter Battery Radar that tracks artillery in flight and marks predicted impact clusters
[*]Origin estimation that narrows repeated artillery fire into counter-battery search markers
[/list]

[h2]Mission Maker Setup[/h2]
[list]
[*]Place the APS module and sync vehicles to it to enable APS on mission start.
[*]Place the Counter Battery Radar module to enable artillery tracking for the mission.
[*]Zeus can toggle CBR globally and toggle APS on individual vehicles during live operations.
[*]APS adds ACE interaction actions for status checks, hard-kill and soft-kill control, voice settings, and anti-drone functions.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires CBA_A3, CORDIS, and ACE.
[*]This beta build is intended for testing, so defensive behavior, effects, and balance may change between versions.
[/list]
